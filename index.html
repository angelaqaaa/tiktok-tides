TikTok Tides:
decoding viral short videos

Our alien scientist just tuned into Earth's favorite time sink: TikTok. Its mission is simple: figure out what makes short videos go viral so it can blend in with humans.

In this lab, "viral" means a short video that reaches unusually high play counts compared to others. To understand why that happens, we will look at sounds, timing, topics, and captions.

A sweet spot around 15 to 30 seconds

Highlight the slices between roughly 15 and 30 seconds. These slices usually form the outer band of the stopwatch. Very short clips under 10 seconds stay closer to the center.

That suggests viral videos in this dataset tend to be short, but not extremely short. There is enough time to set up a moment before the swipe.

Very long clips lose steam
