What we learned

The data shows clear patterns: viral videos tend to use high-energy danceable sounds, mid-length durations (15-30 seconds), focus on top categories, and use hype-oriented positive language. These are tendencies, not guarantees—but they give you a strong foundation for understanding what works on the platform.

Summary and References

What we found

  • Sound: Viral tracks cluster in mid-to-high energy with high danceability. A handful of sounds dominate the platform.
  • Duration: Mid-length videos (15-30 seconds) achieve the highest average views in this dataset.
  • Category: A small set of categories dominate views, with a handful of creators carrying most attention within each.
  • Emotion: Hype-oriented, positive language appears much more frequently in high-performing captions.

What this does not tell you

  • This data covers a specific time period and may not reflect current trends.
  • We focused on views as the primary metric, not engagement depth or longevity.
  • Demographic and geographic factors were not included in this analysis.
  • Virality depends on many factors beyond these four ingredients, including timing, creator reputation, and algorithmic luck.

How to read this

The patterns shown here represent tendencies, not guarantees. These are insights from data, not a recipe that works every time. Use them as guidelines to inform your creative decisions, not as rigid rules.
