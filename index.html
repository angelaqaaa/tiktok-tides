TikTok Tides: decoding viral short videos

What makes a video go viral on TikTok? Using real data from thousands of videos, this interactive story explores the ingredients of virality: sound, duration, category, and the emotional language that pushes trends forward.

Your journey will take you through four key ingredients, each backed by data visualizations. At the end, you'll test what you've learned by building your own viral recipe.

Use the journey map or scroll to move between ingredients.

Ingredient 1

What kind of sound do viral videos use?

Explore a musical universe where songs become planets. Each planet represents an artist, positioned by their sound's energy and colored by danceability.

Reading the chart

  • Planet size: Number of viral songs
  • Distance from sun: Energy (perceived intensity & activity)
    Close orbits = calm vibes · Wider orbits = intense, active tracks
    Technical Definition:
    Energy measures the perceptual intensity and activity of a track. High-energy songs typically feature loudness, speed, fast tempo, and noisy characteristics. Death metal scores high (≈1.0), while soft ballads score low (≈0.0). Based on dynamic range, perceived loudness, timbre, onset rate, and general entropy.
  • Color: Danceability (suitability for dancing)
    Technical Definition:
    Danceability describes how suitable a track is for dancing based on musical elements including tempo, rhythm stability, beat strength, and overall regularity. Values range from 0.0 (least danceable) to 1.0 (most danceable). Calculated from tempo consistency, beat prominence, and rhythmic patterns.

What we learned

The data shows clear patterns: viral videos tend to use high-energy danceable sounds, mid-length durations (15-30 seconds), focus on top categories, and use hype-oriented positive language. These are tendencies, not guarantees—but they give you a strong foundation for understanding what works on the platform.

Summary and References

What we found

  • Sound: Viral tracks cluster in mid-to-high energy with high danceability. A handful of sounds dominate the platform.
  • Duration: Mid-length videos (15-30 seconds) achieve the highest average views in this dataset.
  • Category: A small set of categories dominate views, with a handful of creators carrying most attention within each.
  • Emotion: Hype-oriented, positive language appears much more frequently in high-performing captions.

What this does not tell you

  • This data covers a specific time period and may not reflect current trends.
  • We focused on views as the primary metric, not engagement depth or longevity.
  • Demographic and geographic factors were not included in this analysis.
  • Virality depends on many factors beyond these four ingredients, including timing, creator reputation, and algorithmic luck.

How to read this

The patterns shown here represent tendencies, not guarantees. These are insights from data, not a recipe that works every time. Use them as guidelines to inform your creative decisions, not as rigid rules.
