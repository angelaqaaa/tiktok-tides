Scene 6 — Captions

What words appear in viral captions?

We analyzed captions from viral TikTok videos and extracted the most frequent words. Each word is classified by its emotional tone using sentiment analysis.

Bigger bubbles mean the word appears more often. Words that are semantically similar cluster near each other. Hover on any word to see its connections.

Try clicking on the legend to filter by emotion, or hover on bubbles to explore word connections.

Words like "viral", "trend", "fyp", and "dance" appear frequently, suggesting viral captions often include calls-to-action and trend references. Positive emotions like joy and excitement dominate over negative tones.

Scene 8 — Wrap-up

Recipe for a Viral Short Video

According to our TikTok Tides data

Ingredients

  • 🎵 A sound from a small group of dominant artists or popular tracks.
  • ⏱️ Clip length near the duration band where our stopwatch shows the densest cluster.
  • 📊 A category near the top of our trend pyramid.
  • 💬 A caption tone leaning toward hype or curiosity rather than flat description.
  • 🔗 Connection to communities that use that sound repeatedly.
